# v9.6: VIP Club first in-game version

VIP is an in-game, one-time seven-day **free trial** unlocked at five battle victories.
Features: VIP badge in HUD, status/expiry panel and shortcuts to journal, equipment, expeditions, quests.
VIP does not affect damage, economy, player progression or combat speed, does not spend money,
and cannot auto-renew. The free trial is stored in the existing local save. No new graphics.
Because saves and timestamps are local to the Android device, the offline MVP is not secure
against manipulation and is **not** a real payment entitlement. Any future paid VIP requires
Google Play Billing and server-side receipt verification and entitlement reconciliation.
